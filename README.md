## Profile

I am a **Mining Engineer and software professional** working at the intersection of engineering, technology, and data. My work spans production-grade mobile applications, modern web platforms, and the exploration of data science, machine learning, and AI for practical decision-making.

With a BSc (Hons) in Mining Engineering from the **University of Moratuwa** and research published in **IEEE Xplore**, I bring an engineering mindset to every product: understand the system, simplify the complexity, and build for real-world use.

> Domain expertise shapes the problem. Technology creates the solution.
